## 1. Executive Summary & Budget Rules

### 1.1 The `Footprint × Lifespan` Weighting Model

- `[DECIDED]` Components are prioritized and budgeted by **viewport real-estate share × interactive lifespan** (`Perpetual` / `Episodic` / `Ephemeral`) into a strict three-tier allocation: **Tier 1 (`70%`)**, **Tier 2 (`25%`)**, and **Tier 3 (`5%`)**.
- `[SUGGESTION]` To make tier placement repeatable across future audits, each component is scored on two `1–5` axes (`W = F × L`, range `1–25`):

| Axis | Score `1` | Score `2` | Score `3` | Score `4` | Score `5` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Footprint (`F`)** `[SUGGESTION]` | Micro trigger, popover, banner, toast (`< 4%` viewport) | Compact persistent bar (`2.5rem–3.5rem`) or mid-size sub-editor (`4%–9%`) | Large modal or multi-column card band (`10%–19%` persistent, or `50%–90%` modal) | Major docked pane / split drawer (`20%–49%` area, or `100dvh` expanded drawer) | Primary viewport canvas (`>= 50%` active viewport area) |
| **Lifespan (`L`)** `[DECIDED]` taxonomy + `[SUGGESTION]` scale | **Ephemeral** (`< 5s` dwell: popovers, confirms, banners, toasts, colophon footer) | **Episodic — Low Freq** (`15–60s` setup/rare flow: Command Palette, JSON tab, preset modals) | **Episodic — Moderate Freq** (`15–60s` per build/session: sheet settings, print toolbar, library, sub-editors) | **Episodic — High Freq** (`15–60s+` repeated per session: rolling dice, stepping dice, editing traits/sets) | **Perpetual** (continuously mounted/visible across mode dwell) |

### 1.2 Priority Tiers & Mandatory Agent Guardrails

| Tier | Score Band | Budget Share | Scope & Quality Contract | Provenance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier 1: Critical Canvas & Core Play** | `W >= 15` | **70%** | Primary reading/play/authoring surfaces and core roller workspace. Must maintain zero text/button clipping, strict `0.5rem` (`--space-*`) spacing cadence, responsive `dvh` / `fr` / `%` geometry, WCAG 2.2 AA touch hitboxes (`>= 2.25rem` / `36–44px`), and dark-mode + print parity. | `[DECIDED]` |
| **Tier 2: Active Controls & Authoring** | `6 <= W <= 14` | **25%** | Compact perpetual nav chrome (`AppHeader`, `SessionNav`, `MobileSetJumpStrip`), specialty set bodies, and episodic authoring/outcome inspectors. Must keep structural `--space-*` spacing and reliable state gates (`data-state`, `data-submode`, `v-if`); minimal ornamentation; reuse `.editor-*` and `--bg-surface-*` tokens. | `[DECIDED]` |
| **Tier 3: Utility Chrome & Overlays** | `W <= 5` | **5%** | Transient dialogs, popovers, confirmation prompts, raw JSON tab, duplicate preset pickers, banners, toasts, and colophon footer. Must rely on native `<dialog>` / `[popover="auto"]` and base `--space-*` / `--bg-surface-*` tokens. **Custom CSS polish passes are forbidden unless fixing a verified a11y, focus-trap, or clipping bug.** | `[DECIDED]` |

- `[DECIDED]` **Pre-Flight Tier Declaration Guardrail:** Before editing any `.css` or `.vue` file, every future UI/styling task or agent session MUST declare at the top of its plan:
  `Target Component(s) -> Priority Tier (1 | 2 | 3) -> Permitted CSS Scope`.
  - Tier 3 edits that add bespoke spacing scales, custom color palettes, or decorative animations without a documented layout/a11y defect violate the `5%` guardrail and must be rejected in review.
- `[DECIDED]` **Active Mobile Target Baseline (`DECISIONS.md` 2026-11-13 & Prompt Spec):**
  - Single `3rem` header below `48rem` (`<SessionNav>` hidden; `<AppHeader>` surfaces centered `[popover="auto"]` `#mobile-sheet-menu`).
  - Play jump chrome target envelope: `3rem` header + `2.5rem` jump strip (`--jump-strip-h: 2.5rem` in `css/app.css`, ratified `[DECIDED]` in `design/DECISIONS.md` 2026-11-13).
  - Three-state mobile Play drawer (`data-state="staged" | "split" | "full"`): `staged: 3.5rem` (`--roller-footer-h`), `split: 38.2dvh` (`--split-roller-h`), `full: 100dvh` (auto-collapses `.header`).

### 1.3 Macro Budget Inversion Finding

Comparing actual stylesheet mass (`19,053` lines) against the `70% / 25% / 5%` rule exposes three structural inversions:

1. **`css/print.css` bloat (`4,583` lines / `24.05%` of all CSS):** Houses non-print runtime systems including the **Tier 1** 2D Pillar & Stack Deck (`L3561–3999`, `439` lines), **Tier 2** Template Library Modal (`L2111–2534`, `424` lines), and **dead** `.sheet-toolbar` / `.edit-toolbar` rules (`L4002–4046`).
2. **`TraitSetMini` over-budget (`2,460` Vue lines + `1,109` CSS lines in `css/editor.css:L2948–4056`):** Now a rare **Tier 3** modal (`DECISIONS.md` 2026-11-13 moved live `view.parts` authoring inline into `TraitSetEditorRowMini`), yet its CSS alone equals **`5.82%` of the entire repository's CSS** — exceeding the whole `5%` Tier 3 budget.
3. **Unconditional fixed `.footer` on mobile:** `<AppFooter>` (`3rem` / `48px`) remains mounted and fixed at `bottom: 0` across all non-print screen modes, consuming `5.69%` of Mobile Create (`StudioPanel` closed) and Mobile Roster height while being covered by `.mobile-roller-pill-bar` (`z-index: 950`) in Mobile Play.
